Scope: every screen of the app (Path, Songs, Piece, Player, Theory, Settings), Operate mode, phone first, mouse and
keyboard up to desktop.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, native-feeling practice app in the grammar of Clefs and Flowkey: the labelled keyboard is the hero and each screen has one deep-teal action. Refuses streak-and-mascot dashboards and photo-card catalogues.

OWN-WORLD: Sage-tinted off-white ground (dark: green-black), deep teal primary, mint soft surfaces, amber only for gaps. Onest throughout; chord symbols heavy and large. Round 44px icon buttons, pill segmented controls, a floating glass tab bar, bottom sheets of toggles. Chord-role colours appear on chord tones only.

STORY: The learner sees what to play next, taps once, and plays while the chord and keys read from the music stand.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Path: large title, round settings button; a mint Continue card (title, original title and key, amber gap line, full-width teal Continue); level heading with count; step rows with kind tiles and learned checks; floating tab bar.

FORM: Category standard, owner-pinned by reference products; seed fb31baf0.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the Player's chart strip keeps the current bar centred while the keyboard lights each beat
group as it sounds; sheets and popovers rise with one exponential ease-out.
