Scope: every screen of the app (Path, Songs, Piece, Player, Theory, Settings), Operate mode, phone first and a laptop's
full width (a sidebar and two columns from 1024px), mouse, keyboard and touch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app as a labelled cross-section: the keyboard and the chart are cut open and every key, chord and control wears its printed name. Refuses the dark neon piano app and the sage wellness app.

OWN-WORLD: Paper-white ground (night: dusk brown), warm brown outlines on every shape, even-midtone gouache flats: school-bus yellow for the one action, brick red for what is active, sky blue for a scale and a key played, grass green for learned, sand for sunken. Balsamiq Sans Bold lettering; Onest for reading; a docked bar of coloured icons.

STORY: The learner finds what to play, reads chord and keys from the stand, and presses yellow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Path, phone: lettered title; Continue card, sky header band, yellow Continue; the steps as outlined rows; the docked bar. Laptop: sidebar, Continue beside the steps.

FORM: Busytown cross-section (the bolder-register re-roll's competitive card); seed b355e413.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: a key played fills with its full gouache colour while the marks at rest keep their pale wash,
and its printed label stays on it; sheets rise with one exponential ease-out.

Unresolved: the owner chose this world over its named risk (it can read as a children's book): no characters, animals
or mascots (PRODUCT.md), only the book's colour, line and labelling.
