Scope: every screen of the app (Path, Songs, Piece, Player, Theory, Settings), Operate mode, phone first and a laptop's
full width (a sidebar and two columns from 1024px), mouse, keyboard and touch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app as a labelled cross-section: the keyboard and the chart are cut open and every key, chord and control wears its printed name. Refuses the dark neon piano app and the sage wellness app.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground, a shade darker than its cards (night: dusk brown); a soft 1px line round every card and a 3:1 line round every control; seven faded paints at one lightness, each with a wash: honey for the one action (the only coloured fill on a control), umber for a chosen chip, a card thumb for a chosen segment, sky for a scale and a key played, grass for learned, washes with deep icons naming places. Literata semibold for titles and chords; Onest for reading and controls; a monochrome docked bar. Printed quietly (ADR 0011).

STORY: The learner finds what to play, reads chord and keys from the stand, and presses honey.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Path, phone: typeset title; Continue card, sky-wash header band, honey Continue; the steps as rows with tinted tiles; the docked bar. Laptop: sidebar, Continue beside the steps.

FORM: Busytown cross-section (the bolder-register re-roll's competitive card); seed b355e413.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: a key played fills with its full paint while the marks at rest keep their pale wash,
and its printed label stays on it; sheets rise with one exponential ease-out.

Unresolved: the owner chose this world over its named risk (it can read as a children's book): no characters, animals
or mascots (PRODUCT.md), only the book's colour, line and labelling.
