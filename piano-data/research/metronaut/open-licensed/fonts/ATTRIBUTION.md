How to comply in a web app:
- Ship each font with its OFL text and this copyright line.
- Do not sell the fonts on their own.
- If you modify or subset a font, you must rename it. The Reserved Font Name ("Bravura", "Guido2") may not be the primary name of a modified version. Note: converting to WOFF2 or subsetting counts as a "Modified Version" under the OFL definition ("changing formats"). Simplest compliant path: serve the unmodified OTF, or use Steinberg's official upstream WOFF/WOFF2 builds (github.com/steinbergmedia/bravura) and subset under a new name.
- Fresher upstream versions exist (Bravura is maintained by Steinberg); prefer upstream over these copies.
