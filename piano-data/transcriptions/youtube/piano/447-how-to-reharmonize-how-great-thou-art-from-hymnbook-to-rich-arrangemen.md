## Description

Ever wish you could take a hymn straight from the hymnbook page and turn it into a full, rich piano arrangement—without guessing at chords or “jazzing it up” too far? In this lesson, I walk you step-by-step through “How Great Thou Art” and show you exactly how I move from simple hymnbook harmony to lush, church-appropriate reharmonization using playable voicings and color chords you can reuse in any hymn.

We start by identifying the original chords in the key of B♭ (mostly 1–4–5), then we upgrade the harmony with practical substitutions like major 7, dominant 13, add2, diminished passing chords, minor 11, and slash chords (like E♭/F) to create smooth movement and a fuller sound. I also show you how to use tension + resolution tastefully for a dramatic chorus moment—then finish with a beautiful ending using B♭maj7 and a suspended resolution.
