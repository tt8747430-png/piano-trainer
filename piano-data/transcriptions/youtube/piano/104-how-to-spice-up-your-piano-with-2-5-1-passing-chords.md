## Description

The video is an instructional piano lesson on Introduction to 2-5-1 Passing Chords.
The instructor, Warren, discusses the concept of 2-5-1 passing chords in both major and minor keys.
He explains that in a 2-5-1 progression, the 2 chord is typically diminished in a minor key and a straight minor chord in a major key, the 5 chord is a dominant 7th chord, and the 1 chord is a resolution chord (major in major keys and minor in minor keys).
Warren demonstrates the use of 2-5-1 progressions in different musical contexts, such as harmonizing melodies, transitioning from a 1 to a 4 chord, and transitioning from a 1 to a 6 chord.
He emphasizes the importance of understanding the destination chord when using 2-5-1 progressions.
