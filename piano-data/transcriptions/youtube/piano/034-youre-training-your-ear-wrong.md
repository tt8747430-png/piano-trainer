## Description

Everyone teaches ear training like a race. This is the opposite. The slow, almost stubborn method I actually used to build my ear, one note at a time. Most ear training courses move way too fast. You hear an interval a couple times, you "get it," and then it never really locks in. What worked for me was the opposite of that: strip everything down, take one interval, and spend real time with it until you know it for yourself and not because someone told you. In this video I walk through exactly how I do it: the whole step, the fourth, the minor third, the 10-minutes-on-one-thing practice, and why I think of intervals as feelings and colors rather than music math. It's a little abstract, it might sound insane, but it's how these things finally became permanent for me.
