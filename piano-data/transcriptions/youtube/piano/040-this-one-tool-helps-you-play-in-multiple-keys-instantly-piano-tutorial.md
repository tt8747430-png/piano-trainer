In this lesson, Hear and Play founder Jermaine Griggs reveals one of the most liberating insights in all of gospel piano — that neighboring keys on the Circle of Fifths share the same chords. That means when you move from C major to F major, or from C major to G major, you're not starting from scratch. You already know most of the chords. Jermaine proves this by taking a popular chord progression — the 1-5-6-4 — and showing exactly how it sounds in C major, F major, and G major using songs you already recognize and love.

What makes this lesson so powerful is the shift in perspective it creates. Most pianists treat every new key like a brand new language to learn from the beginning. But once you understand how the Circle of Fifths connects neighboring keys through shared chords, adding a new key to your playing feels like moving into a familiar neighborhood rather than a foreign country.
