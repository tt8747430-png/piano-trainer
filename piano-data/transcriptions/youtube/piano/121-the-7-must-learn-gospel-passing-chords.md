## Description

The video discusses the significance of mastering passing chords for becoming an advanced gospel pianist.
Passing chords is essential for personalizing and enhancing gospel songs beyond their original recordings.
Seven essential passing chords are highlighted: golden, diminished chromatic, minor chromatic, tritone substitution, two five one, first inversion, chromatic median, and secondary dominant.
The course focuses on passing chords in the key of C, demonstrating their application to various gospel songs, and offering practical insights.
The course content includes MIDI files, downloadable sheets, and audio guides to cater to different learning styles and levels.
