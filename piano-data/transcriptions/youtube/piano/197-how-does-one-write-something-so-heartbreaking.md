## Description

Tchaikovsky's 6th Symphony contains one of his most iconic melodies that stands out among the rest for its simplicity in design and yet intense passionate outbursts that characterises Tchaikovsky's expressivity so well. The melody almost seems to outline a V-shape in its form but the general downward trajectory is apparent, strongly resembling a sigh or lament. This is then contrasted quite abruptly with a section that is a lot more rhythmical and lively, almost like a group of musicians who happen to pass by.
