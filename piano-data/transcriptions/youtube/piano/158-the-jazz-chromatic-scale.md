## Description

Chromatic Notes? Here's how Jazz musicians will handle them:

Take your key scale: C major

For scale degrees C E G A (1 3 5 6):
Use a C6 chord (C E G A). 

For D F Ab B (2 4 b6 7): 
Use Bdim7 (B D F Ab).

For the remaining notes (C# D# F# and A#): 
You can use C#dim7 or Cdim7. 

For all the above, use inversions so that the correct melody note is on top. 

This is generally known as the Barry Harris Blocked Chord method, and it is just one of many jazz techniques you can use on a chromatic scale.
