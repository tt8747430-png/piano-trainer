## Description

The tutorial discusses how to build major and minor chords on the piano.
Emphasis on understanding major scales as a foundation for chord construction.
Major chords are built by combining the first, third, and fifth degrees of a major scale.
Minor chords involve shifting the middle note of a major chord down by a half-step.
The tutorial recommends practicing, recognizing, and differentiating between major and minor chords both theoretically and aurally.
