## Description

The first 4 minutes from Chopin’s Sonata No. 3 Op. 58, third movement Largo.

The movement always reminded me of a nocturne. With its graceful, singing line and an arpeggiated serenity that begins at 2:32 is one of the most sublime passages in all of piano. It is absolutely music I would love to listen to at night besides a warm fireplace.

Performance by yours truly, Lisztopher.
