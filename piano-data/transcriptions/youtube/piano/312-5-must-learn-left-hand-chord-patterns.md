## Description

The left hand is often neglected in piano playing, but it is essential for advanced playing, especially in gospel music, to create fuller, richer chords. This tutorial discussed 5 Must Learn Left-Hand Chord Patterns,
The five core left-hand positions: Octave with fifth, adding the ninth, 1 & 7, 1-7-5, and 1-7-3, provide depth to chords and are fundamental for advanced players.
Be mindful of the register when choosing left-hand chords. Lower registers require simpler voicings (octaves, fifths) to avoid muddy sounds.
Combining the five hand positions strategically can greatly enhance your chord progression in gospel songs, adding richness and dynamic control.
