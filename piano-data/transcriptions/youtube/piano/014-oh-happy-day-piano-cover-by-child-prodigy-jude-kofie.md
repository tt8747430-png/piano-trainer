About Jude:
Jude Kofie, known online as Jude Keyz, is a self-taught piano prodigy from Aurora, Colorado. Born with health complications and later diagnosed with autism, he discovered his gift at age ten when his father caught him playing piano by ear with no formal training. His viral story led piano tuner Bill Magnusson to donate a $15,000 grand piano, launching Jude into the spotlight with features on The Kelly Clarkson Show, ABC7, and CBS News, plus a songwriting collaboration with Jason Halbert. Rooted in his family's Ghanaian heritage and gospel influences, Jude shares soulful piano performances across jazz, gospel, and classical music with fans around the world.
