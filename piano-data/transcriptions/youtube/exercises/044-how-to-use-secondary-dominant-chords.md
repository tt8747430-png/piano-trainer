## Description

Want to make your piano chord progressions sound richer and harmonically interesting? One of the most effective ways to transform a simple progression is by introducing secondary dominant chords!

In this practical piano tutorial, Cameron Ward demonstrates how to use secondary dominant chords to add beautiful harmonic movement, tension and resolution to your playing.

Using the popular 1564 chord progression as a starting point, Cameron explains how dominant seventh chords are constructed and how you can introduce secondary dominants as passing chords to create a more colourful and expressive harmonic journey.

Rather than simply moving from one chord to the next, you'll discover how to connect your chords in more interesting ways, introducing new harmonic possibilities without losing the original character of the piano chord progression.

You'll learn:
🎹 How to build dominant 7th chords and understand their harmonic function.
🎹 What secondary dominant chords are and how they work within a chord progression.
🎹 How to introduce secondary dominants as passing chords within a 1 5 6 4 chord progression.
🎹 How to soften the sound of dominant seventh chords through alterations
🎹 How to enrich your harmonic language and create more sophisticated piano arrangements.
This is a hands-on tutorial packed with practical ideas you can immediately experiment with at the piano. 

Try these techniques with your favourite piano progressions and discover just how much musical colour you can create with a few carefully chosen chords!
