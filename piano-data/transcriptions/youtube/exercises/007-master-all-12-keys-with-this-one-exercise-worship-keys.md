## Description

In this tutorial, I'll show you a go-to exercise I've used personally and taught to other worship keys players to build 12-key fluency FAST. The concept is simple: play the diatonic chords of a key in descending order.

99% of worship songs use only diatonic chords — so when you drill the chords that belong to a given key, you become far more confident playing songs in that key. Sounds simple, right? It is. That's the point.

What makes this exercise so practical is that you can scale the musicality to match your level:

- Just getting started? Play basic root-position triads.
- Ready to level up? Use common pro worship chord voicings.
- Want a real challenge? Turn it into a full keys arrangement by adding a melody and harmonizing your chords underneath it.

I'll demonstrate all three levels in the video. If you want to get confident playing in every key, this is the ONE exercise you need to drill — starting today.
