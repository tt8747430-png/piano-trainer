## Description

Have you ever learned a bunch of gospel chords but still felt like your playing was missing something?

The problem may not be your chord vocabulary.

It may be your understanding of chord movement.

Great gospel musicians don't just know WHAT chord to play. They understand where the music is going, how to get there, and what can happen between the chords.

That's where passing chords, walk-ups, walk-downs, turnarounds, bass movement, substitutions, and gospel progressions begin to make sense.

Instead of asking:

“What passing chord should I play?”

Start asking:

“Where am I now, where am I going, and how can I move between those two places?”

That shift can completely change the way you approach gospel piano.

In this lesson, I'll demonstrate why Music Is Movement and show you how understanding chord movement can help you stop memorizing random chords and start understanding the musical principles behind the gospel sound.
