## Description

The tutorial focuses on 6 Ways To Make Your Altered Extended Chords Sound Awesome. Altered extended chords are an advanced concept, not suitable for beginners. Beginners should focus on fundamentals before attempting these techniques.
To use altered extensions effectively, first, learn the song in its original form and understand the melody and chords thoroughly before experimenting with alterations.
Knowing the melody is crucial for using altered chords. The melody guides how chords should be altered and ensures that the altered chords fit harmoniously within the song.
Familiarity with passing chords is essential since altered extensions often involve reharmonization. Also, learning different voicings for the same chord allows flexibility in fitting the altered chords into the melody.
Start applying altered extensions to simple songs before moving to more complex ones. This gradual approach helps in mastering the technique step by step.
