## Description

Want to learn how to play neo-soul chord progressions that sound rich and sophisticated? In this practical piano tutorial, Cameron Ward demonstrates a simple approach to creating beautiful neo soul chords progressions piano players love, without getting overwhelmed by complex music theory.

You'll learn how to play chords using a selection of lush extended chord shapes, including major 9th chords, minor 11th chords, 13th chords, and suspended 13th chords. Cameron breaks each chord down into an easy-to-follow formula, making it simple to understand and apply immediately in your own playing.

If you've ever wondered how to play chord progressions that capture the smooth, sophisticated sound of Neo Soul and R&B, this lesson provides a practical, hands-on approach that you can start using straight away. By focusing on playable shapes rather than complicated theory, you'll quickly develop a vocabulary of beautiful neo soul piano chords and expressive harmonic colours.
