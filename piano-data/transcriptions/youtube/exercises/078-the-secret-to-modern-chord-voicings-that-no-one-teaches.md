## Description

You don't need to play the root, third, and fifth to express a chord. In fact, the most sophisticated, "modern" voicings you've ever heard come from not playing them — from letting the notes of the scale do the talking. In this video, Henrique shares a method that traveled from impressionist painting (yes, Monet) into contemporary jazz — and shows you how to build abstract, beautiful voicings on guitar or piano.
