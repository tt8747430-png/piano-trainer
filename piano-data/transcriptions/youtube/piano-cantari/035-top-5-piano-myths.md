## Description

Tom busts the top 5 piano myths that hold so many players back! In this lesson, you’ll discover the truth behind common myths piano students hear all the time — from whether you need large hands to play difficult music, to questions about piano finger technique, piano practice, and sight reading piano skills.

Do you really need curved fingers piano teachers talk about constantly? Can flat fingers piano playing actually work in some situations? Do you need naturally strong fingers or endless piano exercises to develop great piano technique? And is it too late to start learning if you didn’t begin as a child? Tom answers all of these questions and more in this myth-busting tutorial designed to help pianists of all levels play with more confidence and freedom.
