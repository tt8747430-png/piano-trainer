## Description

Here's one of the best Jazz Piano Comping Exercise To Do Every Day. Works for All Levels. Jazz Tutorial. Piano jazz how to video. We use Autumn Leaves to demo the exercise.

It is 7-step exercise you can practice every day to learn how to comp over Jazz Standards (or any chord progression.) And you can stop on any step (but always start on step 1)
Step 1: 3-Note Word over a Jazz Standard
Step 2:  Word-Group (3rd note shift) over 1 Chord
Step 3:  Word-Group (3rd note shift) over a Jazz Standard
Step 4:  Word-Group (2nd note shift) over 1 Chord
Step 5:  Word-Group (2nd note shift) over a Jazz Standard
Step 6:  Word-Shift (1st note shift) over 1 Chord
Step 7:  Word-Shift (1st note shift) over a Jazz Standard
Don't forget to practice "free play" after the last step.
