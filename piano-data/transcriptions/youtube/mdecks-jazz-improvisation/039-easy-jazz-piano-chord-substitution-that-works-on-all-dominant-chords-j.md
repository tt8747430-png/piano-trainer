## Description

In this jazz piano tutorial we explore a Chord Substitution That Works On All Dominant Chords. We use the symmetric diminished scale (a.k.a. the Half-Whole scale) and pair it with any dominant chord in a jazz tune.
This chord-chordscale pairing offers a very interesting sound and quite a few new upper structures to pair the dominant chord with.
