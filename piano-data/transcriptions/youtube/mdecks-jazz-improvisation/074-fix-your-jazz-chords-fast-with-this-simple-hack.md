Jazz Piano Chords Explained! 🎶 Are jazz chords leaving you scratching your head? Fret not! In this enlightening tutorial, we're unraveling the mystery behind those bewildering jazz chord tensions. 🎹🎵

If you've been overwhelmed by jazz lead sheets stacked with chords like m9, 7#9, b9,b13, maj7#11, 7sus4b9, and 7#9,#11,13, this video is your gateway to conquering them effortlessly.

🤯 Discover the game-changing trick that'll revolutionize your understanding of jazz chords. Our expert instructor breaks down the secret: it's all about the 2 chord. By thinking in terms of the 2 chord, you unlock the door to all those tantalizing tensions.

📚 Building chords with 3rds is the key. Unravel the logic behind chords like Cmaj7 becoming C E G B, revealing the 9th, 11th, and 13th. But wait, there's more! By stacking 3rds from the C major scale, you unravel an array of major intervals, leading to the 2 chord's minor triad: D F A. This minor triad unveils the natural tensions: 9, 11, and 13.

🎶 The magic doesn't stop there! Learn how to decipher the 9, 11, and 13 of any chord, even in complex keys like Eb. Altering these notes elevates your chord game to new heights. Ready for an Eb7#11? Just play Eb7 and reshape your 2 chord into a major triad - F A C - voila, you've got 9, #11, and 13.

🤓 Dive deeper into mastery with a 3-step exercise that solidifies your newfound knowledge:
1️⃣ Train your hands and ears by pairing minor 7th chords with the minor 2 chord. Get familiar with the distinct sounds of each tension.
2️⃣ Elevate your major 7th chords by embracing the #11 - it's the major triad on the 2 chord. Now your major 7 chords feature a 9, #11, and 13.
3️⃣ For dominant chords, work magic with the altered tensions. Play the major 2 chord, but tweak the 9 into a b9, the 13 into a b13, and the 9 into a #9 for the ultimate altered dominant chord.
