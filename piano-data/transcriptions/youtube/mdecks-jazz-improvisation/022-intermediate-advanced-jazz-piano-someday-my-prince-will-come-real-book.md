At this level we take a look at the eighth-note grid, reharmonizations, upper structures and more.

The first thing that you should probably understand about the Real Book, is how important it is to NOT take it so literally. The fact that lead sheets aren’t even dedicated to a specific instrument speaks to how much context is missing and how important it is for the player to provide this context. What I’m basically trying to say is, when you stop looking at the Real Book’s lead sheets as literal depictions of the songs, you’ll begin to make choices that make the music come alive.
