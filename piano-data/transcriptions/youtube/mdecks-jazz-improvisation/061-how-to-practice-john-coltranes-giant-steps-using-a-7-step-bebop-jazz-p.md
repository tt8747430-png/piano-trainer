## Description

In this practice session, you'll learn how to improvise over the bebop classic "Giant Steps" by John Coltrane. I'll take you through the exercise step-by-step. We'll start with the basic chords, and then dive into the specific bebop techniques. "Giant Steps" is such a great and challenging tune to practice with. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player, this video will help you develop your bebop skills and get closer to the sound of one of the greatest jazz musicians of all time. So grab your instrument and let's get to work!

This exercise is based on the following lesson:“Best Jazz Improvisation Exercise To Do Every Day in 7-Steps
Here’s the link to the video:
https://youtu.be/nIBZaOoEp4U

We start with the final challenge which is step-7 in double time (anticipating enclosures)
It is 7-step exercise you can practice every day. And you can stop on any step (but always start on step 1)
Step 1: Broken chords up in root position
Step 2:  Broken chord up in inversions (think of target notes)
Step 3:  Up then down
Step 4:  Down then up
Step 5:  Broken, angular, no need to use all notes in the chord
Step 6:  Chromatic approach to the first note from below
Step 7:  Enclosure (trapping) Diatonic + Chromatic (anticipate)
Don't forget to practice "free play" after the last step.
