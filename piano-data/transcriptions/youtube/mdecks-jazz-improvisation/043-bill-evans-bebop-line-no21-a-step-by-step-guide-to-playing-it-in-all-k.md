## Description

The video is a tutorial on how to play Bebop Line No.21 in all keys. The instructor breaks down the line into a few simple steps and explains the theory behind each step. The tutorial covers techniques like 2-5 in major and minor and variations over a standard progression like Autumn Leaves. The video concludes by encouraging viewers to practice the steps to master Bebop Line No.21 and to watch the next video for further instructions. The instructor also offers a PDF download of the lesson for viewers who are members.

Play the 2-5-1 progression in major using an Am7 arpeggio to the 11th and a descending Mixolydian scale starting on the b7 and landing on the 3 of the Imaj7 chord.
Practice playing these two measures in all keys with ease.
Practice major scales over an Imaj7 chord.
Practice major scales descending starting on the 5th degree over V-I, including the pickup note and landing on the 3rd of the Imaj7.
Practice playing major triad on the 1 chord and minor triad on the 2 chord.
Practice playing m7(9,11) arpeggio.
Replace the root of the ii chord with the enclosure, and descend using the Mixolydian scale to play the first two measures of Bebop Line No.21 in all keys.
