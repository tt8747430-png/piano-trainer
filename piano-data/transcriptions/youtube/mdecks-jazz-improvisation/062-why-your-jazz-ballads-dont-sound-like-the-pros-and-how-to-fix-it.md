## Description

If your jazz ballads sound heavy, muddy, or clunky compared to the pros — you’re probably doing what most self-taught players do: stacking chords with roots in your left hand.
Get the complete course here: https://mdecks.com/voicings-vol1.phtml
The problem? Those roots are the bass player’s job — and when you double them, your voicings lose clarity and motion.

In this video, I’ll show you how to get out of the bass player’s space and make your ballads sound rich, clear, and professional using rootless voicings.

You’ll learn:
How to strip the root and build 2–4 note left-hand voicings from guide tones and extensions
How to connect your chords with voice leading for a smooth, flowing sound
How to use dynamics and rubato phrasing to bring your melody to life
How to apply the same approach to any jazz standard — not just ballads
How rootless voicings free up your hands to add fills and inner motion
Once you master this approach, your ballads will finally have that open, spacious sound that real jazz pianists create — and you’ll be ready to use it in every tune you play.

📍 Pro Tip: If you can hear the bass line in your head, you don’t need to play it. Let your voicings breathe.
