Analyzing the Progression:

Find the Key:
Although Bye Bye Blackbird is in F major, not all songs establish the key clearly. Focus on marking the cadences.
Identify Dominant Chords:
Mark all dominant chords and their resolutions.
Example: C7 resolves to F6 (perfect fifth down).
Brackets and Arrows:
Use brackets for ii-V progressions and arrows for perfect fifth movements.
Example: Gm7 to C7 is a ii-V progression.
Tritone Substitutions:
Mark half-step down resolutions from dominant chords (e.g., F7 to E7).
Roman Numeral Analysis:

Assign Roman Numerals:
Identify chords as I, ii, V, etc., based on their function in the key of F major.
Extended Dominants:
Understand and mark extended dominant sequences.
Complex Functions:
Note that chords in extended dominant chains have ambiguous functions.
