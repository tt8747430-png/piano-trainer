## Description

Have you ever been told to "slow down" when you're under pressure?
It sounds like good advice. But is it always true?

In this reflection, we explore a different perspective: **the opposite of rushing isn't slowing down—it's clarity.**

Through a simple story from Japan, along with examples from Formula One racing, surgery, and martial arts, you'll discover why the most effective people are often able to move incredibly fast without feeling rushed.

Perhaps the real question isn't *"Should I slow down?"* but *"What does this moment require?"*

And perhaps wisdom is not about moving more slowly, but about learning to move with the rhythm of life.
