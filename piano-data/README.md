# Piano data

The owner's source material for Piano Trainer: the books its courses follow, reference apps' screens, scores and
links. Nothing here ships with the app; content is written from it into `src/entities/*/content/`.

| Folder                              | What it holds                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [`method-books/`](method-books)     | The method books whose lessons, patterns and songs the app teaches as courses                      |
| [`courses/`](courses)               | Courses taken as sheets: Vasily Gorshkov's accompaniment course, lesson by lesson                  |
| [`books/`](books)                   | E-books and cheat sheets, by author or publisher                                                   |
| [`scores/`](scores)                 | Sheet music; church songs under `hymns/`, by kind and (arrangements) by meter                      |
| [`reference-apps/`](reference-apps) | Screenshots of other piano apps (and of this one), by app and by screen, numbered in viewing order |
| [`web-references/`](web-references) | Saved links (`links/`, Pianote's under `pianote/`) and images (`images/`)                          |

### Hymns

The owner's church songs (Romanian, Russian, Ukrainian, English, German): PDFs with, where there are any, their
Sibelius (`.sib`), Finale (`.musx`), Encore (`.enc`), MuseScore (`.mscz`), MusicXML (`.mxl`, `.musicxml`, `.xml`)
and MIDI files and recordings. Names are the titles transliterated.

- `hymns/arrangements/<meter>/`: piano and ensemble arrangements by time signature (`2-2`, `3-4`, `4-4`, `6-8`,
  `12-8`); a song with several files has its own folder
- `hymns/arrangements/adam-cand-s-a-trezit-petru-braila/`: Petru Braila's arrangement for strings, with its recording
- `hymns/choir/`: choir arrangements; `m-a-aflat-al-meu-pastor/` holds _Come, Thou Fount_ (its tune) in several keys
  and arrangements (Jason Tonioli, Kaleb Brasee)
- `hymns/songs/`: songs as sheets, choir and full scores
- `hymns/ensembles/`: О. Цапук's arrangements for domra and piano, and cello and piano
- `hymns/hymnals/`: _Песнь возрождения с нотами_, vol. 3; _Боже, спасибо_; the _Free Worship Piano Collection_

`hymns/arrangements/6-8/converted-score.musicxml` is an untitled conversion of one of the 6/8 songs.
