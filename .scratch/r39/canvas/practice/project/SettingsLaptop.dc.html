Up to two actions on each side. Archive and Delete always ask for confirmation.

Songs holds your library: folders first, then songs. Search finds a song by title; Filters narrow the list by key, level or favourites.

New folder groups songs. Folders can contain folders of their own.

Select, or a long press on a row, chooses several songs at once.

Move to… places the selected songs in a folder. Undo in the message reverses it.

Archive puts songs away without deleting them. The Archive button at the top of Songs restores them.

The heart marks a song as a favourite.

View sets how the music is shown: sheet music, chords over lyrics, chord diagrams or a chord grid.

Transpose shifts the song up or down by semitones. Reset restores the original key.

Simplify plays each chord as a plain major or minor triad.

Sound holds the count-in, the metronome and each part’s volume.

Loop repeats a passage. Select it with Shift and a click, or hold a note and drag.

In the tempo menu, Wait mode pauses until you play each note.

A song’s page shows its key, time signature, tempo, chords and sections.

A section’s page holds its lessons: its chords, its chord changes and its patterns.

A lesson opens the Player looping its bars. Play them through in Wait mode to complete it.

Learned marks a song once you know it.

Edit opens the score editor at the current bar; Practise returns to the Player.

Replace overwrites the note at the caret, Insert shifts the following notes, Chord adds a note to the chord there.

The palette holds note values, articulations, clefs, key and time signatures and barlines. The ⓘ beside a section explains it.

Transpose shifts the whole song, its key or its chords in a single step that Undo reverses.

Connect a keyboard by USB or Bluetooth, then choose it with the MIDI button.

Settings › MIDI devices: MIDI input and output, local sound, the device the app listens to, octave shift and a MIDI log of incoming messages.

In Wait mode the Player listens for the notes you play; the Chord finder names the chord you hold.

Practice › Metronome. Set the tempo with − and +, the slider or Tap tempo.

Choose clicks, beeps or a drum pattern, and the time signature. The downbeat of each bar is accented.

Space starts and stops it; ↑ and ↓ change the tempo.

Include what you were doing and what you expected. Reports are anonymous: they carry the app’s version and your device, never your songs.

Connect a Bluetooth MIDI keyboard
  1. 1Power. Turn the keyboard on and put it in pairing mode (see its manual).
  2. 2Mac. Audio MIDI Setup › Window › Show MIDI Studio › Bluetooth, then Connect.
  3. 3Windows. Settings › Bluetooth and devices › Add device.
  4. 4Android. Chrome finds it by itself: press a key once paired.
  5. 5iPhone and iPad. Safari cannot reach Bluetooth MIDI; use a USB adapter.
  6. 6Back here. Press a key: the device appears under Connected devices.
