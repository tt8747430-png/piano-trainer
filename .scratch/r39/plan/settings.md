# Settings (canvas 128)

Premium for Settings means iOS Settings and memory-palaces' SettingsPage: coloured squares to hunt by, values in grey,
one page per group.

## The rulings as they stand

- **Only what is app-wide** (CLAUDE.md): App (General, Storage and backup), Piano (On-screen piano, MIDI devices),
  Library (Selection toolbar, Swipe actions), Support (User guide, Report a problem, About). Each row has its coloured
  square and its value in grey ("Match system", "Not connected", "Up to date").
- **The account first:** the initial, the name, the email and "Synced 2 min ago" with a green dot.
- **Pictures for visual choices** ("these are very heavy to read"): General's Appearance is three small screens
  (Match system split light and dark, Light, Dark), the chosen one ringed in ink; Language is English | Русский.
- **Destructive last, alone, red:** Reset progress at the foot of Storage and backup.
- **Laptop:** the group list in a 372 pane with the account above it, the chosen page beside it in a 704 column.

- **Every page drawn** (canvas 131): Account holds Editorial access (Role, Preview as learner) for editors and
  administrators, Sync (status, Last synced, Changes pending, Sync now), Sign out (asks) and Delete account (asks,
  typed DELETE). Each explanation is an ⓘ beside its group's heading. The Settings pages keep the app's strings
  word for word; Legal shows the app's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
