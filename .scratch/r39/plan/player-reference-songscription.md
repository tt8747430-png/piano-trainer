# The Player: Songscription's score page, as a reference

Source: the owner's saved copy of one Songscription score page (German interface, signed in), sent 2026-11-11 as a
MiroMiro export: a zip of the page with its fonts, icons and the song's recording, and the same page as a JSX element,
an HTML element, and Elementor and Bricks page-builder files. Read for its text and controls only: the render did not
run in this session, so the look is not seen, and the export shows one state of one screen, with no menus opened and
no phone layout. The raw files stay out of the repo: they show the owner's account and carry a recorded song.

Owner, on whether it helps: "will this help you", then "ok document this".

## Proposed for the Player canvas

1. Layout gets a third choice, **Endless tall**: one page that grows downward and fills the window.
2. **Tempo by dragging** the number up or down, with the stepper kept for an exact value.
3. Piano range gets **"The song's keys"**: only the keys between the song's lowest and highest notes.
4. On a laptop, every menu row and transport button **shows its shortcut** beside its name.
5. The Key sheet gets **Reset** when the key differs from the song's own.

Not taken: the piano roll (ruled out 2026-11-05), the upgrade prompts. The assistant is the owner's to decide.
