# R39-5 · Chords, redesigned

Premium here means standing beside Piano Companion's and musicca's chord pages: the chord readable at a glance (name,
spelling, staff, keys), every choice one tap away in a picker with its whole list, and the chord sounding the moment it
changes.

## The rulings as they stand

- "every chord type, fingering by hand and inversion, voicings to choose. Add, add2, add9 named as chords are": the
  Chord picker lists Triads, Added notes (add2, add9, madd9, 6, m6), Sevenths and Extended (9 to 13), each by its symbol
  on the root chosen and its full name; Voicing is a picker of its own; Fingering is a table by hand.
- "Every Practice page has its own Settings page": the gear opens Chord settings (a page on a phone, a popover on a
  laptop) with Arpeggio (Up · Up and down), Staff (Grand staff · Treble only) and the piano's two rows as link rows to
  their home (Play the chord when it changes, All piano settings).
- "No finger numbers on or under keys": the keys show only the chord's colours (right hand honey, left hand violet)
  and C4; the fingers are in the Fingering table, each over its note. Amended by canvas 151: the fingers may be shown on the keys when the learner picks Fingers in the piano's Labels; never by default.
- "A key or root is one of shared/ui/pickers": Root opens the root grid; "No strip hides options": no chip strips.
- "Every control says what it belongs to": every control has a label above it (Root, Chord, Voicing, Inversion,
  Hands).
- "No setting disables or hides another": a voicing the chord cannot take stays in the list, faded, saying why
  ("Needs a 7th", "Needs a chord of 4 notes", "Suits a minor or suspended chord"); nothing else changes.
- Random chords' chords are chosen one by one (canvas 139, "it is still not understandable. what is with this chords only triads and seventh. make proper chords choosing and the chords from and from a key or chords types is also not understandable. and the inversions. make me able to select all the chords and also filter them by type like these scrollable chips on top of the drawer"): Which chords is two rows, Chords (the chosen ones by name, "Cmaj7, Dm7, Em7 +4") and Inversions; Chords from, A key / Chord types and Triads / Sevenths are gone. The Chords drawer holds every chord: scrollable chips on top (All · Chosen · In a key · Major · Minor · Diminished · Augmented · Suspended · Added notes · Sevenths · Extended · Altered) filter one list; each type is a section ("Major seventh · Cmaj7", "2 of 12", Choose all or Clear) of its 12 chords as buttons, chosen ones in the ink fill; "7 chords chosen" and Clear all at the top. In a key shows the key picker and Major/Minor, then the key's triads and sevenths with their numerals, chosen the same way. The drawer keeps its height while filtering. Inversions says which note is at the bottom, each shown on C with that note in honey (C E G, E G C, G C E, B♭ C E G), one or more chosen. Default: the seven sevenths of C major, root position.
- Random chords' chords are a checklist (canvas 140, "I dont like, what is this chose chords. and what is with this show another. and this is a key is also not very understandable"): every chord is a button with a checkbox and its symbol, three across on a phone and four on a laptop; each type's heading is its name ("Major seventh"), "2 of 12" and a Select all checkbox (empty, a dash for some, a tick for all); "7 selected" and Clear all at the top; the chips are All · Selected · Major · Minor · Diminished · Augmented · Suspended · Added notes · Sevenths · Extended · Altered. The In a key chip and Show another are gone; the card at the top is only the example and how the drill works. Replaces the honey tiles, "Choose all" and "chosen" of canvas 139.
- Random chords: chord types and roots, chosen apart (canvas 141, "I dont like this selected. look at the examples of the chords choosing and improve this"): as musictheory.net's and ToneDear's chord exercises (a checklist of chord types and of inversions) and pianochord.org (chords by category, then by note) do. Which chords is Chord types · Roots · Inversions. Chord types is a checklist by group (Triads, Added notes, Sevenths, Extended, Altered), each row the type's name, its symbol on C and a checkbox, each group with "2 of 6" and Select all; chips on top (All and the five groups) stay in place while the list scrolls; "2 types · 24 chords in all" and Clear all. Roots is one octave of the keyboard, every key tapped in or out (the ink fill when in), with All 12 · White keys · Black keys above it. The drill plays every chosen type on every chosen root. Default: Major and Minor triads on all 12 roots. Replaces the chord-by-chord checklist of canvases 139–140.

1. Bar: Back (to Practice), "Chords", the gear (Chord settings).
2. The chord card: the symbol in serif, its full name (with any tensions added), the inversion and voicing, "Also
   written …", the notes spelled; the grand staff beside it.
3. Root (root grid sheet) · Chord (sheet grouped Triads, Added notes, Sevenths, Extended).
4. Voicing (sheet: Close, Drop 2, Drop 3, Shell, Rootless A, Rootless B, Locked hands, So What, each with its one-line
   formula; those the chord cannot take faded with the reason).
5. Inversion: Root · 1st · 2nd (· 3rd for four notes), each with its stack of dots, the bass dot honey.
6. Hands: Right · Left · Both (Both adds the root and 5th in the left hand to a close chord).
7. Fingering: one row per hand, each finger over its note.
8. Colour notes ⓘ: Chord tones; Tensions (tap to add, again to take off); Avoid (struck through).
9. Practise this chord: Arpeggio, Inversions, Chromatic walk, each opening the Player.
10. Play (Stop while sounding) and Arpeggio in a bar of their own above the keys, covering nothing; the keys at the
    foot.
