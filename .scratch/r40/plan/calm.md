## The rulings as they stand

- "refactor further the editor and the songs overview and the songs library, and not just some little tweaks"; "refactor
  the practices pages like we refactored the chords pages in other session artifact": every Songs and Practice page takes
  the shape of the calm Chords page (the other session's "Calm Chords page"). Premium means Apple Music and the Calm
  Chords page itself: one subject in large type, one picture, a quiet switcher, few rows, one floating bar.
- **The page shape (phone):** a bare bar (Back chevron, ♡ where it can be a favourite, ···); the subject large and bold,
  centred, with one soft line under it; one big picture (the keys, the staff, the score, the circle, the gauge); quiet
  text tabs under it where there are views; two or three inset rows (a stepper ‹ value ›, a value ›, a link ›); a
  floating glass bar at the bottom: the main change ("Change scale ⌃", opening a wheel), one control, round honey Play.
  Nothing to scroll.
- **Laptop:** the same page left, the sidebar beside it; a right inspector holds the change wheel inline and the rows; the
  floating bar sits under the picture.
- **Libraries and hubs** (Practice, My songs, Progressions, Accompaniment, Exercises, Quiz) are one calm list: large title,
  search, place chips, inset grouped rows with a coloured tile, the value at the right; two columns on a laptop. The tab
  roots (My songs, Practice) carry a floating glass tab bar on the phone.
- **A song's page:** "Amazing Grace" large; the score is the picture (played bars soft); Score · Chords · Words · About;
  Version ‹ Simple ›, Where you stopped, Song settings; the floating bar Edit · From bar 9 · Play. ··· holds Edit score,
  Move to folder, Export, Publish, Archive.
- **The score editor:** Done · Undo · Redo · ··· and the title; the score page is the picture with the selected note in
  honey; under it the selected note's rows (Pitch ‹ G4 ›, Words under it, Chord above it), or the keys when Keys is on;
  the floating palette: Note value ⌃ (a wheel), Rest, Tie, ♯ ♭, Keys. Laptop: the Note inspector.
- **Fingering** is shown as numbers in a card per hand, never on or under the keys (R39 ruling kept; the calm Chords
  page's Fingering view is to be brought in line when it is ported).
