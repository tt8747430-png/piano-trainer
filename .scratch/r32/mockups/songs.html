The song page

Where: Songs › a song (/songs/hot-cross-buns), phone and laptop.

What changes: the top says what the song is in one glance (cover, who wrote it, one line of facts, the about), the music follows, and Details lists everything about the song as properties: Music, Words, Arranger, Level, Genre, Language, Tags, the book. On a laptop the song fills the left column and its music the right, so the screen is full.

Recommendation: Apple Music’s album page for the top (cover, byline, a facts line, the about clamped with More) and MuseScore’s score page for Details (a plain list of property and value under the music).
2

Editing the details

Where: the song page’s Details, and its cover.

What changes: there is no Edit mode and no Edit song page. A tap on a property edits that one, as in Notion: a sheet on a phone, a popover beside it on a laptop, kept as you choose. Words, Music and Arranger are properties too and write the lines the score prints, so you never leave for the score to change who wrote it. A tap on the cover changes its picture and paint.

3

The song’s Settings

Where: the song’s ⚙.

What changes: Settings only holds how the song lives in your library and how it leaves it: Folder, Favourite (a switch), Duplicate, Archive; Publish, Share link, Export; Delete apart. Edit song page goes, since the page edits itself.

4

Card style

Where: Songs’ More › Card style, a folder’s ⋯ › Card style. A lesson block’s style is the same editor.

What changes, as in memory-palaces: presets first, in one strip; the preview is pinned, a pane of its own between the bar and the controls that never moves and never covers them; a Phone · Laptop switch shows the rows as each will draw them; Reset in the bar, Apply to… and Apply at the foot. On a laptop the library now follows the style: List is the table inside a card in the style, Cards and Grid draw the cards.

5

Groups

Where: Songs › Groups (the tree on a laptop, Shared with me on a phone), a group, an invite link, a shared folder.

What changes: groups could not be made (a server rule refused every new group; fixed). Groups are rows with their picture and paint; a group’s page has its invite card, what is shared with it with Share a folder…, and its members; Join with a link takes a link someone sent you.

6

Where Details stands on a phone

Where: the song page on a phone.

What changes for you: where you find Level, Genre, Tags and who wrote it.

Recommendation: A, Details at the foot, as Apple Music puts an album’s information under its songs: you open a song to play it, so its music comes first.
