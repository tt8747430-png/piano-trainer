One home for the time signature, key and clef

Where: /edit/hot-cross-buns, phone and laptop.

What changes: a tap on the printed time signature, key or clef chooses its bar and opens that palette category; the gear’s Music page (Key signature, Time signature, Tempo, Bars) goes. The tempo is set on its printed mark; bars are added and deleted in Measures.

Built as drawn: Flat’s “click on the time signature in the score, or use the Time signature section”, one control.
2

The gear: what the score does not print

Where: the editor’s ⚙.

What changes: precise names, ordered by frequency of use, each with a one-line description; no intermediate pages.

3

Only what applies, never greyed

Where: the palette, every category.

What changes: a tool that cannot act on what is chosen is not drawn; Write as common or cut time only for 4/4 and 2/2; From this bar · Whole song only where they differ.

4

Piano keys on a phone

Where: the editor’s bar, phone held upright.

What changes: Piano keys stands in the bar before Play, as on the laptop and as Flat’s keyboard button.

Your call: the title

What changes for you: where the title and composer are typed. A opens one Title and credits page from the title (Flat); B keeps the title typed in place.
