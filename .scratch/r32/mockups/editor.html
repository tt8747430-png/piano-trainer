The palette: every section in one scroll

Time signature: tiles, then Customize

Where: the palette’s Time signature, and the time signature printed on the score.

What changes: six tiles as Flat’s (common and cut time are tiles); Customize opens the Time signature and Pickup wheels with ✗ ✓. Nothing else inline.

3

Key signature and clef: bare tables

Where: the palette’s Key signature and Clef.

What changes: no Major · Minor, no names in the cells, no whole-song switch, no This bar · Until the end · Entire staff. A selection limits the change, as Flat does.

4

Tempo in Measure

Where: the palette’s Measure, and the tempo mark printed on the score.

What changes: Tempo, accel., rit. and a tempo move from Text to Measure, as Flat has them.
