Settings and Administration · Round 32

Settings rebuilt end to end: two panes on the MacBook Air that scroll on their own (as Songs’ tree and list do), every page pushed with a working Back on the iPhone, preferences moved to where they belong, About and the legal pages fixed, the Administration area folded into Settings, and every word rewritten. Each screen has a box: Looks right, Change it or Not needed, and a note.

Connecting the feedback store…

1

The list and the sidebar

Where: Settings (/settings) on the iPhone, and every Settings page on the MacBook.

What changes: on the iPhone, Settings is one grouped list — General, Piano, Library, Support, and Administration for an administrator — each row pushing its page with Back. On the MacBook, the list becomes a compact sidebar that stays in place and scrolls on its own; the page beside it scrolls with the window, and the page shown is highlighted. View as a learner leaves the list (question 4).

Built: macOS System Settings’ sidebar and this app’s Songs tree (17rem, sticky under the bar, its own scroll). The heavy phone cards no longer stand in for a sidebar.
2

Selection toolbar and Swipe actions

Where: Settings › Library › Selection toolbar, Swipe actions (were General › Select toolbar, Swipe actions).

What changes: both become Settings pages of their own in a Library group, as memory-palaces and iOS Mail keep them, so the sidebar marks them and Back works; their editors gain a heading, a one-line footnote and room to breathe.

3

About, Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Where: Settings › Support › About, and the legal pages it opens.

What changes: About is a Settings page like the rest (the sidebar beside it on the MacBook, Back on the iPhone). It opens with the app and its version, then Software update and the licence, then Legal (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service as full rows), then Acknowledgements. The legal pages go back to About.

4

Preview as learner, and Report a problem

Where: Settings › Account (editors and administrators), and Settings › Support › Report a problem.

What changes: the learner preview moves from the foot of Help to the account it depends on (A), and the Administration bar’s graduation-cap button goes, so there is one way to it. Report a problem becomes a page with its field in sight.

Recommendation: A. The preview exists because of the account’s role; Notion and Linear keep role-bound tools with the account and the administration area, never among Help.
5

Administration

Where: Settings › Administration (/admin, each section /admin/…), administrators only.

What changes: the pop-up of nine sections becomes ten pages: a list on the iPhone, the sidebar’s Administration group on the MacBook. Feedback is split from the error log. Removing an administrator, deleting a group or a flag and suspending a publisher all ask first in the app’s one confirmation.
