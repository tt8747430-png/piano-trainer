The piano · Round 33

One on-screen piano, drawn and played as a real one, on every screen that shows keys: the Player, the score editor, the Chord finder, Chords, Scales, Intervals, Progressions, lessons and the trainers. Phones in the iPhone 17 Pro frame, laptops in the MacBook Air with Safari. References: musicca.com’s virtual piano, OnlinePianist, Recursive Arts’ A-23 and Midiano.

Each screen has Approve, Revise or Remove and a note; your answers stay in this browser.

1

Keys drawn as a piano’s: ivory, ebony, felt and depth

Where: every on-screen piano. Shown on the Chord finder (/practice/chord-finder) and the Player (/play/hot-cross-buns).

What changes: the white keys are ivory, lit from your side, with a front lip; the black keys are ebony, bevelled, with a sloped front and a shadow on the whites; a red felt strip runs along the far end. A key you press sinks and lights: sky blue for a plain key, the mark’s full colour for a marked one (right hand, left hand, a wrong key in red, a key only the pedal holds ringed). On a computer each key shows the letter that plays it as a small key cap.

Recommendation: as drawn. OnlinePianist’s depth and felt, musicca’s legible labels, with our marks unchanged so lessons and the Player read as before.
2

The pedals: on the lyre beneath the keys, or a lit Sustain toggle

Where: the rail beneath every on-screen piano.

What changes: the sustain behaves as a real one. Held — by a finger, the space bar, a MIDI pedal or the score while the music plays — every key you strike or release rings on; when it lifts they stop together, the bass dying slower than the treble. A key struck again under the pedal sounds afresh. A tap latches it, lit by an indicator light, until the next tap. The soft pedal (una corda) makes each key quieter and darker; the sostenuto holds only the keys down when it is pressed. The settings sit at the rail’s end and never scroll out of sight.

Recommendation: A, the pedals on the lyre — a piano’s pedals where a pianist expects them, kept from Round 32, now with the latch’s indicator light (the A-23’s Sustain light). B (OnlinePianist’s lit toggle) is compact but shows the sustain alone; soft and sostenuto have no place in it.
3

Piano settings in place, from the rail’s ⚙

Where: the ⚙ at the end of every piano rail. In the Player, the ⚙ keeps opening the Player’s settings at On-screen piano (your ruling); it shows the same options.

What changes: ⚙ opens a sheet over the screen instead of leaving for Settings, with what matters while playing, as musicca’s Settings: Key size, Note names, Volume (new), Pedals, and on a computer Play with computer keyboard. All piano settings opens the full page. Every option has one home: the sheet and the page show the same controls.

Recommendation: as drawn. Recording stays the score editor’s Record and marking keys the Chord finder’s, so neither is repeated on the keyboard. A choice of piano sounds waits for a second recorded piano.
