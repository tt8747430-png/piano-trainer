Your own folders in Practice? And the states

Where: Exercises, Accompaniment and Progressions.

A question for you: Songs lets you make folders. Practice’s libraries have the books, Favourites and Mine. Should you also be able to make your own folders there? Below them, the empty Favourites and no-match states, the same on all three.

Recommendation: A, not now. The books already sort the material, Favourites keep what you return to, and Mine holds what you made. B adds moving and nesting for items you rarely have more than a dozen of.
