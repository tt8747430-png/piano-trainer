The view switch

Where: Songs (My songs · Explore) and Learn (Lessons · Courses), the same control.

What changes: the small switch under the bar, which left half its row empty, becomes the bar's title: two tabs, the current one in ink.

Recommendation: A. The App Store’s Today · Games, and Spotify and Instagram’s tabs, put a place’s views where its title stands; nothing extra is drawn, and the row is full.
2

Filters, and songs not in a folder

Where: Songs › Filter, and the laptop’s tree.

What changes: Filter gains Folder (Not in a folder) and Progress (Learned, Not learned yet). What is on shows as chips under the search; the tree gains Not in a folder.

3

Card style

Where: a folder’s ⋯ › Card style, and Songs’ More › Card style. A lesson block’s style uses the same editor.

What changes: the preview no longer hangs over the controls. On a phone it has its own pane at the top and the controls scroll beneath it; on a laptop it has its own column. Corners, Border and Shadow are one row each of choices, not five words in a cramped switch or a − + stepper.

4

The song page and its Settings

Where: a song’s page, its ⚙ Settings, and Export.

What changes: ✎ beside the title opens the score editor. The song page itself (cover, details, about, tags) is edited from Settings › Edit song page. Settings are grouped: the song, Library, Share, Progress, and Delete apart. Share link appears once.

5

Menus

Where: a song’s ⋯ and a folder’s ⋯ (and the same as a swipe and on the laptop’s table).

What changes: each menu starts with what you open, then what you change, then sharing, then what removes; groups with separators.
