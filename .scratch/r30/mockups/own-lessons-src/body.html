Your lessons and courses, Round 30

Learners write their own lessons and courses. Drawn as whole screens on the phone (iPhone 17 Pro, 402 × 874) and the laptop (MacBook Air 15″, 1710 × 904), scaled down, with the app’s real lessons and courses around them. Each screen has a box: Looks right, Change it or Not needed, and a note.

1

Your lessons, after the levels

Where: Learn › Lessons, the chip after Professional (/?journey=own).

What changes: a fifth chip, Your lessons, lists the lessons you wrote, with how far you read each, and New lesson as the last row. New lesson asks a name and opens the lesson editor, the same one the app’s lessons are written in. Your lessons never count towards a level.

Recommendation: as drawn. A chip keeps your lessons one tap away without lengthening a level’s page; Practice’s libraries put Mine last the same way.
2

Your courses, after the shelves

Where: Learn › Courses, at the end.

What changes: Your courses follows the Method books, each course on its cover (a picture on a paint, as a Songs folder has), its lessons and how many are learned. New course asks a name and opens the course editor.

3

Your lesson’s Settings

Where: /lessons/own-lesson-1/settings, from the gear in the lesson’s bar.

What changes: the same page as an app lesson’s, with what you can do to your own: Rename, Duplicate, Reset progress, and Delete last, asked first. Share link is left out until sharing is built: the lesson lives on your devices.

4

Editing your course

Where: /courses/own-course-1/edit, the pencil on your course’s page.

What changes: the course editor the app’s courses are written in, with your course’s own parts: its title and line written in place, its Cover, and its lessons dragged into order. Add lesson finds one of yours or one of the app’s (added by reference: it stays the app’s, and its progress is the same wherever you read it), or makes a new one. Done saves; there is no Publish.
