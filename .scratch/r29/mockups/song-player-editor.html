A song, its Player and its editor

How a song's page, the Player and the score editor would look once round 29 is built, with every Settings page each one opens. These are iPhone screens, plus a laptop where the layout differs. Each screen has a number, and the line under it says where it opens from.

The rules every screen follows:

  • One gear per screen, opening that screen's own Settings.
  • ✎ Edit sits beside the title.
  • No ⋯ menu and no setting inside a menu.
  • Every setting has one home.
  • Settings pages use Flat's cards (question 2 on the other page): a choice among three or more is a card with every option in sight, and on/off is a switch.
