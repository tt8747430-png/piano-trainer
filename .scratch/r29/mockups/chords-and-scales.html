Loading where your feedback is kept…

Under every screen: tap Looks right, Change it or Not needed, and write what to change. It saves as you type.

Two rules both pages follow Floating actions: one capsule, centred at the bottom. Play, the main action, is always on the right and filled honey. The second action (Arpeggio, the tempo) is on the left. Same order on every page. Only what fits: each chord shows only the choices that make sense for it, following the chord dictionaries. A triad offers added tones (add2, add9, 6…), and only a dominant 7th offers alterations (♭9, ♯11…). A choice that doesn't apply is hidden, not greyed.

Chords

From top to bottom:

  • the chord's name, big;
  • the choices that build it: root, size, chord, then only the extras it can take;
  • the chord written on a staff and on the keys, with fingers.

Arpeggio and Play float at the bottom. How the chord is laid out (inversion, voicing, between the hands) has its own row.

2

Scales and keys

You choose a scale ("D major ⌄" in the bar opens the picker). The page has three tabs:

  • Scale: how to play it.
  • Chords: the chords made from its notes, each with a preview, fingers and its own options.
  • Key: D major as a home key for a song. Its signature, the keys you'd move to, chords you can borrow, progressions and songs in it, and how to harmonise a melody note.
