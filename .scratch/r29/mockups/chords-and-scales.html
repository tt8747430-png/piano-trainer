Scales and keys

Scale or key? A scale is a row of notes you practise: how to play D major up and down. A key is a home note for music: a song "in D major" uses D major's notes and is built from its seven chords. So the page has two tabs: Scale (to play it) and Key (to understand music in it: its chords, how to harmonise a melody, its neighbouring keys, its progressions). Scales with no key of their own (pentatonic, blues) have no Key tab.
