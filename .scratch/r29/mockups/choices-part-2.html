The Player: bar, view picker and its one Settings page

This follows your Flat and Chordify screenshots. ✎ Edit sits beside the title, MIDI and one gear are at the end of the bar, and there's no ⋯ or Setup. The tools row reads Sheet ⌃ · Transpose · Simplify. The gear opens one Settings page whose rows open their own pages.

My recommendation: build it as drawn. The score editor uses the same bar, with Done in place of ✎, and its own gear.

2

How every Settings page is built

You said you don't like how the Settings pages are made. Here is one page, the Player's Playback, built three ways. Whichever you choose, every Settings page in the app gets built that way: the app's, the Player's, the editor's, each Practice page's, a folder's and a song's.

My recommendation: B for choices among three or more, a plain switch row for on/off. Every option is in sight with its example, as in Flat, and nothing is cut short on a phone.

3

The app's Settings, with Computer keys inside On-screen piano

Only what concerns the whole app. As you asked, Computer keys is now a section of the On-screen piano page, shown on a computer only. On a laptop it's two panes.

4

Picking a key: Flat's key-signature grid

This is Flat's grid from your screenshot: each cell is the signature itself, drawn in the music font. D♭ (5 flats) and C♯ (7 sharps) are separate cells, so no key needs two names. Here it is in New song's last step, beside the time signatures, and on a Practice page.

My recommendation: the grid wherever you choose a key (New song, the editor, Scales and keys, the Chord player's Settings). On Chords and Intervals you choose a root note, not a key, so that uses the keyboard with a spelling switch.

5

Chords: size first, then the chord

As you asked: the size first (Triad · 7th · 9th · 11th · 13th), then only the chords of that size. The word "Quality" and the 7th switch are gone. Play and Arpeggio sit together but look different. "Right hand from" is now "Top note".

My recommendation: A. You see every chord of that size at once and change it with one tap. The same Size and Chord rows appear in Scales and keys' Chords view.

6

Scales and keys: switching between Scale, Chords and Key

The bar stays one height, and the page's name is the choice itself ("D major", tap to change). The three views need a switch. One way works like the Player's view chip, the other is a row of tabs inside the page.

My recommendation: B. With only three views, tabs inside the page fit any phone and stay in sight. A chip suits the Player, which has four views and needs the room for the music.
