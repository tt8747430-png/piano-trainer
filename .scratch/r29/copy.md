# R29 B2: copy changes

## Meaning changed
- **Privacy › Children (new):** an account needs the learner to be 13 or older; a child's account is deleted.
- **Privacy › Questions, Terms › Questions (new):** contact is Settings › Help › Report a problem (a new row
  sending to the app's error reports; no address shown, the plan's default).
- **Terms › What you publish (rewritten):** adds that each publish confirms the title, composer and the rights
  to share digital and changed versions (D3); that publishing lets the app show the item in the Public library;
  that a report is read by the editors and the item taken out if it holds.
- **Terms › Other people's work in the app (new):** names Verovio, Noto, Salamander (CC BY 3.0), Splendid
  (public domain).
- "Last changed" moved to 4 November 2026.
