# Plan — Verovio engraves every score (replaces VexFlow)

Decision: ADR 0078. Every staff the app draws (Player, score editor, lessons, explorers, Chord finder) is engraved
by Verovio 6 (LGPL, loaded unmodified as its own chunk) from MEI written by `scoreToMei`. VexFlow, `engrave.ts`,
`vexflow-notes.ts` and our spacing port (ADR 0077) go once nothing uses them.

## Shape

- `shared/lib/notation/mei.ts` — `scoreToMei(score, options)`: staves (sung ones, the piano's braced), layers,
  chords, rests (`r-<staff>-<tick>`), notes (`n-<staff>-<tick>-<i>`), chords (`c-<staff>-<tick>`), measures
  (`m-<index>`), ties across bars, beams by the beat, triplets, key/time/clef changes, barlines, repeats, endings,
  tempo, marks (artic, fermata, ornaments, arpeggio, grace, slurs, dynamics, hairpins, octave lines, pedal,
  expressions, fingers), segno/coda/jumps; options: title, staves shown, fingers, the time before (meter hidden
  where it continues), chord symbols as `harm` (whole-score pages only; lines keep our HTML labels).
- `shared/ui/score/verovio/engine.ts` — one toolkit, lazily imported (`verovio/wasm`, `verovio/esm`); in a browser
  in a module Web Worker (`verovio.worker.ts`), in tests called directly. `render(mei, options) → svg`.
- `shared/ui/score/verovio/svg-layout.ts` — `layoutOf(svg, score, staves) → ScoreLayout` read from the SVG's own
  coordinates (staff-line paths, notehead `translate()`s, the definition-scale viewBox and page-margin), no browser
  layout: works in jsdom.
- Options for a line: `pageWidth = cssWidth × 100 / scale`, `scale` from our staff size (a staff space of 10 px at
  scale 1 is Verovio scale ≈ 55.6), `breaks: 'encoded'`, `minLastJustification: 0` (justified), `adjustPageHeight`,
  no header/footer, margins 0 but the brace's, `svgViewBox`; measure numbers hidden (our labels number bars).
- `ScoreView` keeps its API; selection marks `g.note` ids in a tick span; muted staff and note names by CSS/SVG.

## Steps (commit each)

1. MEI: the rest of the marks and options, tests. ✔ basics
2. Engine + svg-layout + ScoreView on Verovio; ScoreView tests.
3. Editor sheet, Player lines and pages; overlays checked (caret, clicks, drags, selection, loop, cursor).
4. Every other ScoreView user: lessons, explorers, Chord finder, TappableStaff, ChordTrail.
5. Remove VexFlow, engrave.ts, vexflow-notes, spacing port, measure-width estimates if unused; PWA precache for
   the Verovio chunk; full test run; ADR 0078; CLAUDE.md.
